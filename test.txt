The old lighthouse on Graystone Point had been silent for years, but tonight the lamp flickered to life.

Mira climbed the narrow stairs with a lantern in hand, her breath clouding in the cold night air. She had come because the town's children kept whispering about a voice calling from the sea. At the top, she found a brass box half-buried in dust.

Inside was a tiny compass, still warm, and a note in her grandmother's handwriting: "When the light returns, follow the star that points home."

Mira stepped onto the balcony and looked out across the dark water. One star glimmered brighter than the rest, drawing a shining line toward the horizon. She set the compass in her palm, and it spun once before settling due west.

The sea answered with a soft glow beneath the waves. A small boat drifted toward shore, carrying a single lantern and a figure waving. Mira smiled as the lighthouse beam swept the harbor, and for the first time in years, the town's silence felt like a promise.

From then on, every winter night, the beacon glowed brighter, and the children swore they could hear the ocean singing a welcome home.
